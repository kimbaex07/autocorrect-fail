## The entry

> I texted my boss 'I quit' but autocorrect changed it to 'I quilt' — now I'm teaching a sewing class on Friday.

**Character count: 110** (limit: 1–199)

## Why it lands

The slip is a single word — "quit" becomes "quilt" — and it is exactly the kind of swap autocorrect makes: one letter apart, a real word, no typo gibberish. The wrong word flips the whole message: a dramatic resignation turns into a wholesome hobby announcement. And the punchline sells it — instead of storming out of the office, the sender is now stuck teaching a sewing class on Friday. The joke is clear on first read, with no explanation needed.
